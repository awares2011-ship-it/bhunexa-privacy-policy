BhuNexa – Privacy Policy

भूनेक्सा – गोपनीयता धोरण · Effective date: 6 November 2026

1. About this policy

This policy explains how the BhuNexa (भूनेक्सा) Android app and website (“the app”, “we”) handle information. BhuNexa helps people in Maharashtra view publicly available land information, such as the Property Card (मालमत्ता पत्रक), the Bhunakasha plot map and the 7/12 and 8A extracts. It also offers weather, farm news, scheme information and land-measurement tools.

BhuNexa is not a government app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or an official app of the Government of Maharashtra or any government department. Land records come from official public portals, and their copies are for information only. For legal use, obtain the digitally signed copy from the official service.

2. Summary

  • No sign-up or account is needed.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your personal data for advertising.
  • We do not run a server that stores your land-record searches, mobile number or CAPTCHA.
  • Your settings, history and downloaded files stay on your device, and you can delete them at any time.

4. Information used automatically

Location (optional)

Location is used only when you tap “My location” (weather, map or GPS land measurement). Approximate coordinates are sent to the weather service (Open-Meteo.com) to fetch the forecast. Location is not stored on any server of ours and is not used in the background.

News and publisher logos

Farm news headlines are loaded from Google News RSS. To show a publisher's logo, the publisher's website name is sent to a public icon service (Google or DuckDuckGo favicon services). No personal data is included.

Analytics (optional, off by default)

If analytics is enabled in a release, it records only anonymous event counts (for example “weather_open”) with coarse context such as the district. It never records names, mobile numbers, survey numbers, coordinates or device identifiers. You can switch it off in Settings.

Crash and technical logs

The app does not include third-party crash reporting or advertising SDKs. Google Play may give us aggregated, anonymous crash statistics under Google's own policy.

5. Notifications

BhuNexa can show local notifications for news, schemes, weather tips and app updates. These notifications are planned on your device. You choose the categories, and each category sends at most one per day, with quiet hours at night. You can turn any category, or all notifications, off in Settings or in Android settings. Notifications never claim to be official government messages. This version does not use a push-notification server.

6. Data stored on your device

  • Language, home district, notification choices
  • Recent searches (see section 3) and cached district/taluka/village lists, which make forms load faster
  • PDF/PNG files you save, in Download/BhuNexa, and the download history
  • Mobile number, only if you choose “remember this number”

To delete all of this, use Settings → Clear saved data, or uninstall the app. Files you saved to Download/BhuNexa stay until you delete them.

7. Permissions

  • Internet: to load official portals, maps, weather and news
  • Location (optional, only while you use it): for weather, the map and GPS measurement
  • Notifications (optional): for the notifications described above
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only): to save PDF/PNG files to Downloads

8. Sharing, security and retention

We do not sell or share personal data with third parties for their own purposes. Requests to official portals and services use HTTPS (encrypted in transit). Because we do not keep your personal data on our servers, there is nothing for us to retain or delete there. Your on-device data stays until you clear it.

9. Children

BhuNexa is a general utility for land information. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Important changes will also be shown in the app.
